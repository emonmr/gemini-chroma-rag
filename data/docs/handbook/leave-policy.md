# Leave Policy — Nimbus Coffee Co.

*Nimbus Coffee Co. is a fictional company. These documents exist so you can test that answers come from retrieval and not from the model's training data.*

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive **22 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata, based on their contracted hours.

Leave accrues monthly (about 1.83 days per month). New starters can take leave after completing their first 60 days.

Up to **5 unused days** can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used before **31 March**, otherwise they expire.

## Requesting leave

Requests go through the HR portal ("Bean Counter"). For absences of three days or more, submit the request at least **two weeks in advance**. Your line manager approves or declines within 3 working days.

During peak season (1 November to 15 January), at most two people from the same café team may be on leave at the same time.

## Sick leave

Employees get **10 days of paid sick leave** per year. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note is required. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive **16 weeks** of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive **4 weeks** of fully paid leave, which can be taken any time within the first year after the birth or adoption.

## Public holidays

Public holidays are paid in addition to annual leave. Café staff who work on a public holiday receive double pay plus a replacement day off.
