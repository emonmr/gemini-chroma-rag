# Remote Work & Equipment — Nimbus Coffee Co.

## Who can work remotely

Office-based roles (engineering, marketing, finance and HR) may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. Café and roastery roles are on-site by nature.

Every team has one **anchor day** per week when everyone is in the office. Engineering's anchor day is **Tuesday**; marketing's is Thursday.

Working from another country is allowed for up to **20 working days per year**, with your manager's approval and a check by HR for tax rules.

## Home office stipend

New office-based employees receive a one-time **home office stipend of 450 USD** for a chair, desk or monitor. Receipts must be submitted within 90 days of your start date.

An additional **30 USD per month** is paid towards internet costs for anyone working remotely at least two days per week.

## Laptops and security

Engineers get a laptop with 32 GB of RAM; everyone else gets 16 GB. Laptops are replaced every **3 years**.

All devices must use full-disk encryption and the company password manager. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within **24 hours** at it-help@nimbus.example.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are **10:00 to 15:00** in your local time zone. Outside core hours, people are not expected to answer messages.
