# Nimbus Home Brewer X2 — FAQ

## What is the Brewer X2?

The Nimbus Home Brewer X2 is a pour-over coffee machine for home use. It brews 1 to 8 cups and has a built-in burr grinder with 18 grind settings. The water tank holds 1.2 litres.

## What temperature does it brew at?

The default brew temperature is **93 °C**. It can be adjusted between 88 °C and 96 °C in 1-degree steps from the companion app. Lighter roasts usually taste better at the higher end.

## How do I descale it?

Descale the X2 **once a month** if you have hard water, otherwise every three months. Use one sachet of Nimbus Descaler mixed with 1 litre of water, then hold the **Cup** and **Steam** buttons together for 5 seconds to start the 25-minute descaling cycle. Run two tanks of clean water afterwards.

## The display shows error E7. What does that mean?

**E7** means the grinder is jammed — usually by oily beans or a small stone. Unplug the machine, remove the bean hopper, and clear the burrs with the included brush. If E7 comes back, contact support.

Error **E3** means the water tank is not seated correctly.

## What is the warranty?

The Brewer X2 has a **2-year warranty**, extended to **3 years** if you register the machine in the app within 30 days of purchase. The warranty does not cover damage caused by not descaling.
